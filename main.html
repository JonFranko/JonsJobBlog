The Series So Far

Part I: Corporate Reality (Essays 1-7) documented the gap between stated corporate values and lived employee experience—from culture fit masking conformity to DEI programs abandoned under budget pressure to oversight systems too weak to hold institutions accountable.

Part II: The Human Cost (Essays 8-15) translates those structural failures into human experience—neurodivergent masking, enforced silence, hostile feedback systems, coercive severance, disability inclusion theater, bureaucratic betrayal, wellness theater, and the distance that makes it all invisible.

Part III: Recovery & Rebuilding (Essays 16-22) is the reconstruction—purpose after burnout, humane metrics, the professionalism trap, community as counterweight, transparency as self-defense, dignity across geography, and the long argument for keeping a record of your own.
